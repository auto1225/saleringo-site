Integrations

What connects today.
And what doesn't.

Most vendors answer with a wall of logos and let you assume. The question that matters is narrower: does it talk to the system your business actually runs on — and if not, what does your front desk do instead? Here is the answer, in one place, with no dates we can't keep.

New here? Start with the two-step tour — about a minute  →

Live todayConnected and in use. Set up during onboarding, no extra charge.
In buildBeing worked on. We won't give you a ship date, because we'd rather not miss one.
Bridge for nowReaches your system through Zapier, a webhook or a CSV export — not a direct link.

Calendars & scheduling — the connection that matters most

Google CalendarLive today Two-way. The AI reads real availability before it offers a slot, and writes the booking back with the customer's details attached. Changes and cancellations sync too.
Outlook CalendarLive today Same as Google Calendar — availability checked before anything is offered, booking written back on confirmation.
Saleringo's own calendarLive today If you don't run a shared calendar at all, use ours. Opening hours, buffers, per-staff availability and per-service durations are configured during onboarding.

Channels — where the conversation arrives

Your existing phone numberLive today You keep your number. Forward all calls, or only the ones you don't pick up in a few rings — and turn forwarding off with one dial code any time.
Website chat widgetLive today One script tag. Works on any site, including builders you don't control the code of, and it's usually the safest place to start.
WhatsApp Business APILive today Official API. Meta's per-message template fees are billed through at cost — see pricing for how that works.
KakaoTalk business channelLive today For Korean-market customers and cross-border businesses selling into Korea.

Getting records into what you already run

WebhooksLive today Every event — new inquiry, booking made, quote sent, stage change — can be pushed to a URL you choose, the moment it happens.
ZapierLive today The practical bridge to several thousand tools. This is how most businesses connect a system we don't support directly yet.
CSV exportLive today Included on every plan, not an add-on. Conversations, customers, bookings, quotes and reports. Your data leaves whenever you want it to.
PDF & Excel reportsLive today The dashboard and channel reports export as documents you can hand to an accountant, a partner, or a board.

HubSpot · SalesforceBridge for now On the roadmap. Contacts, conversation summaries and stage changes push over Zapier or webhooks today, which for most teams is enough.
Anything currently in buildIn build Nothing sits here today. When a direct link is genuinely being written it appears in this row — still without a ship date, because a date we miss is worse than a gap we named. If a row above matters to you, say so and it moves up the order.

Why there are no dates on this page. The roadmap items above are real work we intend to do, in an order decided by which customers need which system. A month next to each one would make this page more persuasive and less true. Ask us on a call where yours sits, and we'll tell you what we honestly know.

And why "bridge for now" is written plainly. A bridge means somebody on your team does a step manually, or trusts an automation to do it. That is a real cost — and the most common reason software like this quietly stops being used. If your business can't absorb that step, you should know before you pay, not after.

You may not need any of this

The integration nobody talks about is the one you skip.

Many of the businesses this is built for never got a CRM to stick. Inquiries live on a pad, in a phone, in somebody's head. If that's you, there is nothing to integrate — Saleringo's own CRM is the system of record, and the whole question disappears.

  • →No system today? Start with ours. Pipeline, bookings, quotes, customer records and reports come with the plan.
  • →A system you like? Keep it. The answering layer feeds it and Saleringo stays out of the way.
  • →A system you hate? Run both for a month. Move when you're sure, not because we asked.
