Why we built this

Night inquiries were
dying in the inbox.

Saleringo started with a venue owner's morning routine. Every day she opened her inbox to the same scene: messages sent at 11 PM, 1 AM, 2:47 AM — couples asking about dates, travelers asking about rooms, homeowners asking if anyone could come tonight. By the time she replied at nine, most had already booked with whoever answered first. Nothing was wrong with her business. Something was wrong with the hours. So we built the employee she couldn't hire: one that answers every inquiry the minute it arrives, says only what she approved, and wakes a human when it matters.

Somewhere it is already 11 PM, and the question is being asked anyway.

How we work

Three principles,
non-negotiable.

01

Verified answers only

The AI speaks only from facts you approved — each with a source and an expiry date. Two conflicting prices? It stops answering and tells you, instead of picking one and hoping.

02

Humans in the loop

Handoff is a feature, not a failure. Emergencies, contracts, clinical questions, and judgment calls go to a person — with the full conversation attached, never a cold start.

03

Honest limits

We publish what works today, not a roadmap dressed up as the present. Usage is billed as usage. When the product changes, this website changes with it.
