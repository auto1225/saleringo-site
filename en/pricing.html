Billing FAQ

Tax, refunds & payments

Before you enter a card

The questions owners actually ask us first.

Not "what does it cost" — that's printed above. It's tax, refunds, what happens on a failed payment, and whether we quietly stop answering. Straight answers below.

14 daysfull refund on your first payment, no exit call required
Do prices include tax?

No. Tax is calculated on your invoice based on your billing country. If you have a VAT/GST number, give it to us before the first invoice and it goes on the invoice. Tax is shown as a line on the invoice, never added silently afterwards.

What currencies can I pay in?

Prices are listed in USD. You can be billed in USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, SGD, KRW or JPY — tell us which one before your first invoice. Tax is added to your invoice based on your billing country, and your VAT or GST number goes on it. If your local currency isn't one of the seven, you are billed in USD.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at your next billing date.

What happens if I cancel?

Your workspace stays active until the end of your billing period. After that you have 30 days to export your data before deletion.

Do you offer refunds?

Within 14 days of your first payment, in full. After that, we don't refund partial periods, but you can cancel any time.

What happens if a payment fails?

We retry for 7 days and email you. After that your workspace enters a 7-day grace period. We never silently stop answering your customers without telling you first.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Setup is self-service. Guided knowledge-base onboarding is an optional paid add-on, quoted before any work starts.
