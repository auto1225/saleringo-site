What counts as a conversation? One customer, one channel, one 24-hour window — however many messages go back and forth inside it. If the same customer writes again three days later, that's a new conversation. A phone call is one conversation plus its per-minute voice usage. Handoffs to your team don't end the conversation or start a new one.

How WhatsApp is billed: messages inside an included conversation are covered by your plan — no per-message charge. Only conversations beyond your allowance are metered: $0.08 per conversation plus $0.02 per message inside it. Meta bills separately, and per message rather than per conversation — that changed on 1 July 2025. When a customer writes to you, replying costs nothing from Meta for the next 24 hours; outside that window you must send an approved template, and Meta charges for each one it delivers. Marketing templates are always charged; utility and authentication templates are charged only when they land outside that window.     Billing FAQ

    Tax, refunds & payments

    Before you enter a card

    The questions owners actually ask us first.

    Not "what does it cost" — that's printed above. It's tax, refunds, what happens on a failed payment, and whether we quietly stop answering. Straight answers below.

    14 daysfull refund on your first payment, no exit call required
    Do prices include tax?

    No. We are a Korean company with no tax registration elsewhere, so the only tax we add is Korean VAT, for Korean customers only. Outside Korea your invoice carries no tax. Give us your VAT/GST number before the first invoice and it goes on the invoice. Tax appears as an invoice line, never added silently afterwards.

    What currencies can I pay in?

    Prices are listed and billed in USD. The one exception is Korea: Korean businesses are billed in won, at the prices on the Korean site. There is no third currency. Tax works as above: Korean VAT for Korean customers, no tax elsewhere — where reverse charge applies you account for it yourself, and the VAT or GST number you give us goes on the invoice.

    Can I change plans later?

    Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at your next billing date.

    What happens if I cancel?

    You keep the service through what you already paid for; the unused part of the month is refunded by date, or you simply stop at the period's end. Afterwards you have 30 days to export your data before deletion. Card billing charges the same day each month until you cancel; nothing renews after that. Bank transfer is invoiced first and paid on receipt — nothing is taken automatically.

    Do you offer refunds?

    Full refund within 14 days of your first payment. After that, cancelling refunds the unused part of the month, counted by date. Usage you have already run — talk minutes, message volume — is charged at the listed rates and is not refunded.

    What happens if a payment fails?

    We retry for 7 days and email you. After that your workspace enters a 7-day grace period. We never silently stop answering your customers without telling you first.

    Is there a setup fee?

    No. Setup is self-serve by default; the five founding teams in this round write their first answer set together with us, and the free build before you buy is a recording of the AI answering ten of your own questions.
