Two minutes, no account.

No account needed, wherever you are. Fill this in and it is recorded; a person confirms within one business day. Nothing is charged on this screen.

1Where you are

Your country sets the currency, how tax is handled, and which number goes on the invoice. Pick it first and the figures below follow.

This decides the tax treatment — and whether the order can finish on this screen. Individuals and public bodies continue as a written order.
Only if it differs from the country we invoice.

2What you are taking

You can change this later. Plans move up or down monthly, with no penalty.

3Where the tax invoice goes

If you are in Korea the registration number is checked as you type, because the tax invoice needs it. Everywhere else it just goes on the invoice, so it is optional. One exception: an EU business that leaves its VAT number blank continues as a written order.

This goes on the tax invoice or invoice exactly as written.
Left empty, it goes to the contact email above.

4How you will pay

Both are billed monthly. There is no term, so you can stop at any time.

5Confirm

Check the amount on the right (below, on a phone). Everything marked [required] has to be ticked. Choosing card adds one more, for the recurring charge.
