WhatsApp questions, answered

Before you ask.

Can I keep my existing WhatsApp number?

Yes. We connect the number your customers already have saved to the official Business API — or you can start with a new one. One honest caveat: moving to the API means leaving the WhatsApp Business app, and old chat history does not migrate. We walk you through the switch during onboarding.

How long does template approval take?

Meta reviews every template. In our experience most are approved within a business day, though some take longer or get rejected on wording. That is why we draft and submit templates for you — reminders, confirmations, order updates — and handle any rejections until they pass.

Can I send marketing blasts?

No — and that is deliberate. Saleringo is built for answering inquiries and sending transactional notifications: reminders, confirmations, updates. Bulk promotional broadcasts damage your number’s quality rating with Meta and violate spam policies, so we do not do them.

Which countries does it work in?

The WhatsApp Business API works in most countries where WhatsApp operates — which is most of the world. Since 1 July 2025 Meta charges per template message delivered, not per conversation. The rate depends on the template’s category and the customer’s country. Replying inside the 24-hour window a customer opens costs nothing.

What happens when the AI does not know something?

It says so, and hands the thread to your team with the full conversation attached. Every answer it does give traces back to a fact you approved, with a source and an expiry date — so it will not invent a price, a policy, or an availability that does not exist.

How long does setup take?

Business verification with Meta is the slow part and it is out of our hands — it usually takes a few days. On our side, we build your knowledge base with you and draft your first templates in the same week. You approve every fact before anything goes live.
