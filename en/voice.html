How your number connects

Three ways to forward.
Zero new numbers.

Call forwarding is a standard carrier feature — a dial code, not a port. Nothing changes on your bill, nothing changes on your business cards. Pick the mode that fits how you actually work, and switch it whenever you like.

MODE 01

Forward after-hours only

Run the day yourself. At closing time one dial code sends calls to your AI line; at opening they come straight back. The night shift you never had to hire — the most popular start for home services and clinics.

Typical setup · *72 → your AI number at close, *73 at open
MODE 02

Forward when busy or unanswered

Your phone still rings first. If nobody picks up within about 20 seconds — or you’re already on another call — it rolls to the AI instead of voicemail. You catch the overflow without changing a single habit.

Carrier feature · busy / no-answer forwarding, ~20s ring delay
MODE 03

Forward always

The AI becomes your front desk. Every call answered, the routine handled — hours, prices, service area, bookings — and the rest live-transferred to your team with the caller’s context already captured.

Front-desk mode · unconditional forwarding, human handoff on request

Do I keep my existing phone number?

Yes. Nothing is ported and nothing changes on your bill. You set up call forwarding with your current carrier — usually a short dial code — and your AI line answers behind it. Turn it off the same way, any time.

Will it understand my callers’ accents?

It handles a wide range of English accents, including Australian and Southern US speech — but we don’t promise perfection. During onboarding we test it against recordings of your real callers, and it goes live only after it passes. When it isn’t confident it understood, it asks the caller to repeat, or hands the call to a person.

What happens when it doesn’t know the answer?

It says so. The AI answers only from the fact base you approved; anything outside it gets “let me have someone confirm that for you”, followed by a booked callback or a live transfer. It is built to be boring and correct rather than confident and wrong.

Does it speak Spanish?

Yes. English and Spanish are native-quality voices, and the AI detects the caller’s language from their first sentence — no menu, no “press 2”. Beyond those two, further languages are supported with standard voices.

Are calls recorded — and do callers know?

Every call is recorded and transcribed so you can review exactly what was said. A disclosure line plays at the start of each call, and you control both the wording and how long recordings are kept. Recording rules vary by country and state — during setup we flag what typically applies where you operate, and you confirm it with your own local guidance.

Can I listen before it goes live on my line?

Yes, and we insist on it. Before forwarding is switched on we run test calls to a sandbox number with your fact base loaded, and you sign off on the recordings. If the voice or the wording isn’t right, we change it and test again.

How long does setup take?

The forwarding itself takes minutes — same day. The real work is your fact base: prices, hours, service area and escalation rules, which we build with you and you approve line by line. With the founding cohort that typically takes a few days. The AI answers nothing until you have signed off.
