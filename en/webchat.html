Questions we actually get

Before you paste the line.

Will it slow my site down?

No. The widget loads asynchronously after your page renders, weighs about 40KB, and never blocks your content. If our CDN were unreachable, your site loads without the widget — nothing breaks and nothing errors on your page.

Does it detect the visitor's language?

Yes. It detects the language a visitor writes in and replies in that language. Your approved facts stay in one source language; translations are generated per conversation, and the lead record notes which language was used so your reply can match.

How does the handoff to a human work?

When a question falls outside your approved facts — or hits a topic you flagged, like contracts, payments, or medical advice — the widget tells the visitor a person will follow up, collects their contact details, and notifies your team by email and in the Saleringo inbox with the full transcript. During business hours, your team can also take over the chat live.

What data does it store, and where?

Conversation transcripts and the lead fields you configure. No cookie is set for visitors who never open the widget. You choose the data region at setup, you can delete a conversation or a whole lead record from the dashboard, and deletion removes it from the knowledge base too. Full detail is on our Security & Trust and Privacy pages.

Can it run alongside my existing chat widget?

Yes. Set Saleringo to the opposite corner and run both while you evaluate — many teams do this for a couple of weeks. When you switch, removing the old widget is the only change; your Saleringo install stays the same three lines.
