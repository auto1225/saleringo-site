Every trade has a room
where the money walks out.

Saleringo is an AI that answers your phone, your website chat and your WhatsApp after you close, and files what it hears into a CRM already shaped for your trade. That shape is the pack: its fields, its stages, its automations and the one thing it will never answer. Twenty-five trades have one — pick the room that looks like yours.

What a pack actually is

A pack is not a chatbot personality.
It is a CRM with your trade's fields already in it.

The furnace dies at 2 AM. Your voicemail is not a plumber — and the homeowner is already dialling the next number on the list.

0 call-out fees invented. The after-hours rate is read off the price list you approved, or it is not quoted at all.
What it answers at 2:47 AM
01"Do you cover my ZIP code, and what's the after-hours call-out fee?" — from your approved price list, not a guess. 02"Can someone come tonight?" — offers the emergency slot or holds tomorrow's 8 AM first call. 03"Water is coming through the ceiling." — flags the emergency and rings your on-call tech instead of answering.

Three questions, and the third one is why a plumber buys this: the emergency reaches a person while the caller is still on the line.

Health & care

A clinical question the AI must refuse, and a diary that has to fill anyway.

  • Dental→ New patients and recalls. The chair is full, the desk is not — and the call about an implant quote comes in at 7:10 PM. 0diagnoses the AI is allowed to make
  • Clinics & med-spa→ Multilingual appointment intake with a hard rule: no clinical advice, ever. Anything close to it goes straight to your staff. 0medical claims the AI is allowed to make
  • Veterinary→ Boosters, estimates and nurse clinics booked out of hours — while toxicity, dosage, symptoms and end-of-life go straight to a vet. 0clinical judgements the AI is allowed to make
  • Senior living & care→ The enquiry line answered at any hour and your published fee schedule read as written — with a named person put on the line for anything clinical or safeguarding. 0clinical or safeguarding calls the AI handles alone

And two that are not trades

  • Selling across time zones→ Inbound travel, international students, export sales. When your customers wake up as you sleep, the trade matters less than the hour.
