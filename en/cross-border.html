Where we draw the line

On medical inquiries, the AI does logistics — never medicine.

Saleringo handles guidance and booking: prices you approved, availability, interpreter arrangements, directions, what to prepare. It does not give clinical advice, assess suitability, or discuss outcomes. Those questions go to your medical staff, every time, with the full conversation attached.
