Automatic booking

It doesn't offer to book.
It books.

The AI collects what a booking actually needs — date, time, service, party size, which room or which practitioner — checks it against your live calendar, and holds the slot while the customer is still talking.

  • →Your rules, not ours. Opening hours, buffer times, double-booking limits, which services need a longer slot.
  • →Changes and cancellations too. A caller moving an appointment updates the calendar and alerts the staff member affected.
  • →Confirmation goes out immediately on the channel the customer used, with a reminder before the day.

Automatic quoting

The quote that used to
take two days.

Your prices, your options, your discount logic. The AI takes what the customer asked for, runs it through the rules you set, and produces a real estimate — ready to send, or ready for you to check first if that's how you'd rather work.

  • →You keep control of price. The system never invents a number. If a case falls outside your rules, it routes to a person instead of guessing.
  • →Send or review. Auto-send for standard requests; hold for approval on anything above a threshold you set.
  • →Every version is kept against the customer record, so you can see what was offered and what was accepted.
