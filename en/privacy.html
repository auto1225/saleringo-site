§ 1

What we collect

We collect the minimum needed to run Saleringo and talk to you. Concretely:

  • Account data. Name, work email, business name, industry, and country — what you give us when you request early access or create an account.
  • What you choose to send us. The early-access form on this site doesn't post to a hidden server; it opens an email in your mail app. We receive exactly what you decide to send — nothing more.
  • Usage data. Which features are used, error logs, and basic browser/device information — so we can keep the product working and decide what to fix first.
  • Billing data. Payments are handled by a payment processor. We see that you paid and for what plan; we do not store your card number.
This website itself: no advertising trackers, no third-party analytics pixels, no cookie wall hiding twelve "partners."
§ 2

What we don't — your customers' data

When your business runs its front desk on Saleringo, your end customers' conversations — the calls, chats, and WhatsApp messages — belong to your business, not to us.

  • We act as a processor. We process those conversations on your instructions, to answer inquiries, qualify leads, book appointments, and update your records — and for no other purpose.
  • We don't sell personal data. Not yours, not your customers'. There is no "may share with selected partners" clause hiding here.
  • We don't run ads and we don't build advertising profiles from anyone's conversations.
  • You control the settings. As the controller of your customers' data, you decide what is collected in conversations and how long it is kept — see Security & Trust for how the roles work.
§ 3

AI & training

This is the section most AI vendors bury. Here it is in three sentences:

  • No cross-customer training. Your conversations and your knowledge base are never used to train or improve models for other customers. Your competitor's assistant learns nothing from your data.
  • Improvement is opt-in. Using your own data to improve your own assistant is off by default and happens only if you turn it on.
  • Approved facts stay yours. The verified answers you sign off are your content. We don't reuse, republish, or resell them.
Why this matters: Saleringo's whole design is that the AI speaks from facts you approved — not from patterns soaked up across customers. The privacy policy and the product architecture are the same promise.
§ 4

Retention & deletion

  • While your account is active, we keep the data needed to run the service — account details, settings, your knowledge base, and conversation history per your retention settings.
  • When you leave, close your account or email hello@saleringo.com. We delete your account data within 30 days, and purge it from backups within 90 days.
  • Your customers' conversations follow the retention period you set as controller — when it lapses, they go.
  • Your rights, wherever you live. You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We honor these requests without making you cite a statute.
§ 5

Contact

Privacy questions go to hello@saleringo.com — a human, not an autoresponder, replies within one business day.

Saleringo is operated by Honest Marketing Co., Ltd. (South Korea) — serving customers worldwide. If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top changes with it, and material changes are announced by email.

Where GDPR fits

For customers established in the UK or the EEA, you are the controller of your customers' personal data and Saleringo acts as your processor. Our Data Processing Agreement sets that out, incorporates the Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers outside the EEA, and names each subprocessor and the country it operates in. Request it at hello@saleringo.com and we send it before you sign anything.

Nothing on this page limits rights you hold under your own country's law. Where local data-protection law gives you more than this policy describes, the local law governs.
