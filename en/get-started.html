The questions people actually ask before saying yes

Fair questions.

Will it sound like a robot and embarrass me in front of my customers?

It's the first thing you should test, and we won't ask you to take our word for it. Call our own line outside office hours and you'll hear exactly what your customers would hear — we run it on ourselves. In the free build we send back, you also get a recording of it handling your ten most-asked questions. If it isn't good enough for your business, that will be obvious in about ninety seconds, and you'll have lost nothing.

What happens when it doesn't know the answer?

It stops and gets a person, with the whole conversation attached so your team isn't starting cold. It answers from the facts you approved and nothing else — it does not improvise a price, a date, or a promise. That restraint is the reason it can be trusted on your main line at all. How that works →

How much of my week does setup take?

About thirty minutes of your attention, spread across the first few days — reviewing answers and correcting them. We do the building. There is no setup wizard to sit through and no configuration project. Setup itself is free on every plan; if you'd rather we sat with your office manager and built the knowledge base together, that's an optional service we quote before any work starts.

Will it work with the software I already run?

Sometimes fully, sometimes partly, sometimes not yet — and we'll tell you which one you are before you pay, not after. Calendars are the most reliable connection; industry practice-management and booking systems vary a great deal. See the honest status list →

Almost nobody uses this yet. Why would I be early?

You'd be early, and we're not going to dress that up. What we can do is make being early cost you less than it costs us: the founding cohort gets 50% off for three months, the 14-day refund applies to the plan fee, and there is no contract to exit. When the first customers agree to be named, their names and their numbers get published — not before. Until then, judge us on the build we do for your business, which costs you nothing.

What if I want to stop?

You cancel, monthly, without a call. Your data exports on the way out — conversations, customers, bookings, everything. Turning off call forwarding takes one dial code on your carrier and your phones behave exactly as they did before us; we'll show whoever runs your front desk how to do that during setup, so it never depends on reaching us.

Where are you, and who would I be dealing with?

Honest Marketing Co., Ltd., registered in South Korea and building for customers worldwide. The number on this page is our real main line. Email reaches a person, not a queue. More about us →
