In plain English

Six answers,
before any of the jargon.

You do not need an IT person to read this part. If you own the business and you are the one who would have to explain this to a worried customer, these six sentences are the whole story.

1 Your customers’ details sit apart from every other business we work with.

Not in a shared pile with a label on it — separated. And we don’t take that on trust: an automatic check runs before every update we release, and the update does not ship if one business’s records can be reached from another’s.
2 It is locked while it travels.

Everything moving between your customers, your screen and us is scrambled on the way, so it cannot be read in transit. An unlocked connection is refused rather than allowed through.
3 We use it to do your work, and nothing else.

Answering, booking, quoting, writing the customer record — on your instructions, for no other purpose. Inside our own team, reaching the live system needs a second login step, is limited to the people whose job requires it, and is written to a log.
4 Your conversations never teach an AI that works for somebody else.

We do not use one business’s conversations to train the AI serving another. Using yours to improve the model at all is off unless you turn it on in writing — and you never have to.
5 You can take it all with you, or have it deleted.

You decide how long things are kept. Ask for an export or a deletion and we carry it out — that is a written obligation in the agreement, not a favour you have to argue for.
6 Nobody finds out afterwards that they were talking to an AI.

Every call and chat opens by saying so, with a way to reach a person in the same breath. You can reword it; where the law requires it you cannot switch it off. Recording follows your country’s consent rule automatically.

How data is processed

Your data.
Your instructions.

Clear roles make every other security question easier to answer. Here is who controls what when your front desk runs on Saleringo.

C Your businessController You are the controller of your end-customer data — the inquiries, contact details, and bookings your customers share with you. You decide what is collected, why, and how long it is kept.
P SaleringoProcessor Saleringo processes that data on your instructions — to answer the inquiry, book it against your calendar, build the quote from your price list, write the consultation log, and move the customer along your pipeline. For no other purpose.
§ Written down, not impliedDPA For UK and EEA customers, this is the GDPR relationship: you are the controller, Saleringo is your processor. Because we operate from South Korea, transfers out of the EEA are covered by the Standard Contractual Clauses inside our Data Processing Agreement, which also names every subprocessor and the country it runs in. Ask for it while you are evaluating — you get it before you pay, not after. These roles, our obligations, and our subprocessors are set out in that agreement — see Documents below.
Security measures

What we do —
and only what we do.

Each item below reflects the current implementation. When something changes, this page changes with it.

Tenant isolation

Every customer's data is separated with row-level security at the database layer. Cross-tenant isolation tests run in CI on every release before it ships.

Encryption in transit

All traffic between your browser, your customers' channels, and our APIs is encrypted with TLS. Plaintext connections are not accepted.

Access control

Multi-factor authentication for administrators, least-privilege roles by default, and audit logs of administrative access to production systems.

Data regions

Choose where your end-customer data is stored: EU, US, and APAC region options are available on request — talk to us before you sign, because we scope it per customer rather than selling it as a plan tier.

Retention & deletion

Retention policies are set per organization. We honor export and deletion requests for your data — the controls a DPA and privacy regulations expect.

Incident response

A defined incident process: detection, severity classification, containment, and a commitment to notify affected customers without undue delay.

If you are a healthcare, legal or financial business, read this before you go further.

Regulated industries have a specific question — HIPAA and a Business Associate Agreement in US healthcare, and its equivalents elsewhere — and that question has to be settled in writing before a single patient or client record reaches us. We are not going to put a badge on this page to make you comfortable, for the same reason we do not publish certifications before the audit report exists.

So raise it in your first email, not your last. Tell us that a BAA — or your jurisdiction's equivalent — is a condition of purchase, and you will get a written answer before you pay us anything, rather than a discovery after you have. If the answer is not the one you need this quarter, you will have it early enough to go elsewhere, and we would rather lose that deal than sell around the question.

What is already true is written above this box, and none of it depends on a certificate: your data is isolated per customer and that isolation is tested on every release, connections are encrypted in transit, production access requires multi-factor authentication, your conversations never train anything that serves another business, and you can export or delete everything on any day without asking us. Separately, the AI is configured to refuse clinical questions outright — diagnosis, medication, suitability and symptom assessment always go to your staff, never to an answer. AI & Recording Policy

The question
every buyer asks in 2026.

Who knows they're talking to AI, what gets recorded, and what your data trains. Here are the answers, in full.

Disclosure

How Saleringo discloses AI

Every conversation begins with a clear statement that the customer is talking to an AI assistant, with a visible way to reach a human. You can change the wording. You cannot turn it off in countries where disclosure is required.

Recording

Call recording

Recording rules differ by country — some require all parties to consent. Saleringo applies your country's rule automatically and records the disclosure and consent basis with every call.

Training

How your data is used for AI

Your business knowledge and conversations are used to answer your customers. We do not use one customer's data to train models that serve another customer. Model improvement using your data is off by default and requires your written opt-in.

Second one, first sentence

Nobody finds out later.

Disclosure is the opening line on every channel, in the caller’s language, with a way to reach a person in the same breath. You can reword it; you cannot switch it off where the law requires it.

Kept, and kept apart

One tenant, one wall.

Recording follows your country’s consent rule automatically, and the consent basis is stored with the call. Your conversations never train a model that serves another business.
