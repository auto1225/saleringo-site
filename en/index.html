The 2:47 AM problem

The furnace dies
at 2:47 AM.
Your competitor picks up.

Nobody schedules an emergency. The AC quits on the hottest night, the pipe bursts on a Sunday, the guest lands at 11 PM. Whoever answers first gets the job — and it is almost never the business that was asleep.

How the home-services pack takes a night call  →

A nine-to-six desk is closed for 15 of the day’s 24 hours — then both weekend days on top of that. Across a full week the phone is answered for 45 of 168 hours. Every hour in the lit part of that dial is an hour a customer can still decide to call somebody, and 2:47 AM is one of them.

This is not a statistic we collected. It is what a nine-to-six week divides into.

The lights are on upstairs. The call still lands in voicemail.
