Tutoring FAQ

Asked by directors.

Does it really work with WhatsApp?

Yes. Connect your existing WhatsApp Business number during setup — official WhatsApp Business API, approved templates, 24-hour messaging window handled. Parent messages on WhatsApp and on your website flow into one inbox, the AI answers both from the same approved fact library, and any handoff reaches your staff in the same thread the parent started.

Can it book placement tests into our actual schedule?

Yes. You define the slots the AI is allowed to offer — specific days, times, and capacity per test session. It only proposes open slots, books directly into your calendar, and sends the parent a confirmation and a reminder. Change your slots anytime; the AI updates instantly.

What about exam-season inquiry spikes?

Seasonal peaks are exactly what an AI front desk is for — it answers every parent at once, at midnight if needed. Plans include monthly conversation allowances with alerts at 80% and 100%, so you can add capacity before the summer or exam-season rush. We never silently stop answering your parents.

We run multiple locations. Is there a plan for that?

Yes. The Scale plan covers multi-location operations under one contract: a shared, HQ-approved fact library with per-location overrides for schedules and tuition, location-level inboxes, and a headquarters view across all sites. See Pricing for details.
