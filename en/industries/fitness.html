Where the conversation stops

A question about a body
is not an objection. It is a stop.

Studios screen before a first session for a reason, whether the form on your wall is the PAR-Q+ maintained by the Canadian Society for Exercise Physiology, the Health Commitment Statement distributed by ukactive, or one your insurer wrote for you. So the screening is not a line the AI reads past. An injury, a condition, a pregnancy, a medication, a recent operation — the response is always the same, and it is not an answer. The words go down verbatim, a qualified instructor calls, and nothing resting on that question gets confirmed. It ships enabled, and it is not a setting anyone can switch off to hit a trial target.

No view offered "Which class is best for my knee?"

It names no class, suggests no modification and does not say "that should be fine". It writes the knee down in the caller's own words, says a qualified instructor will ring, and books nothing that rests on the answer.

Routed to an instructor "I'm 14 weeks pregnant — can I keep coming?"

Never assessed, never reassured, never softened. Pregnancy, injury, surgery and medication are a permanent handoff: the record is written, a named instructor takes the callback, and the place is held rather than confirmed.

Hard boundary No training, nutrition or rehab advice. No money decisions.

It will not build a programme, judge a pain, suggest what to eat, or promise a result by a date. It will also not cancel a membership or waive a fee — those are the studio's decisions, and it has no authority over either.

STEP 01Ask before the dateFirst session with you, or a screening older than your window? The question comes before a class is offered.
STEP 02Send your form, not our wordsYour own health questionnaire goes out on the same thread. The AI does not paraphrase a single line of it.
STEP 03Held, not confirmedThe place stays held until the form comes back, and the caller is told which of the two it is.
STEP 04Record it verbatimAnything said about a body goes on the record in the caller's own words, with a named instructor attached.

Second rule, same shape: most studios set a minimum age, and a guardian rule under it. Your policy is applied exactly as written and never interpreted: the AI asks the age, reads your rule back, and sends anything that does not fit to a person. How answers get approved →

Two studios. Three. A group.

Riverside does not quote
Northgate's timetable.

Two sites under one owner almost never run the same timetable, the same capacities or the same membership. Each site is set up as its own studio inside one account, and the AI never crosses between them.

01Its own number, hours and price list

A caller asking about Riverside hears Riverside's classes, its own membership rate and its own closed day. Never the other site's prices, in either direction.

02Its own rooms, capacities and instructors

Bookings land in the timetable of the instructor who actually teaches there. Somebody who covers both sites keeps one timetable across the two, so she cannot be put in two 6:15s at once.

03Each manager sees only their own site

The manager opens the board and sees their enquiries, their held trials, their waitlists and their outstanding screening forms — not the group's.

04You see all of them, side by side

One report, every site in the same row: enquiries answered, trials booked, trials attended, class occupancy against capacity, late-cancel rate. Where the rooms are earning, and where they are not.

FAQ

Studios like yours ask us

Somebody asks which class is safe for their knee. What exactly happens?

It gives no answer, because it has no business giving one. It does not name a class, suggest a modification or say the words "that should be fine". It writes down what the caller said about their knee in the caller's own words, tells them a qualified instructor will ring them, and books nothing that depends on the answer. Anything touching a body is a permanent handoff here, not a confidence threshold.

Can it actually book the trial, or does it just take a message?

It books it. The trial goes into a named class on the real timetable, against the real capacity, with a place held in the caller's name. It states what your trial includes and what it does not, sends your health screening form on the same thread, and shows the place as held until the form comes back.

Does it know when a class is full?

Yes, and it will not invent an eleventh bed. A class at capacity gets a waitlist place in order, and the caller is told which position they are in. When somebody late-cancels, the waitlist is offered in order and withdrawn from everyone else the moment it is taken.

Will it cancel a membership or waive a no-show fee?

No. It reads your notice period and your fee rule exactly as written, records the request with the date and time it was made, and routes it to the person who processes it. Ending a membership and waiving money are decisions the studio makes, and the AI has no authority over either.

Can I start with only the hours when a class is running?

That is how most studios begin. Forward the line for the 6 AM block and the evening block, read the transcripts for a week, then widen it when the answers sound like your studio. Going back is a forwarding change at your end, not a support ticket.
