Eight fields, and one of them stops the calendar

A generic CRM asks for a name.
This one asks who is on the other side.

  • →Matter type, from your own practice list. Most inquiries fail here, and the ones that fail late are the expensive ones. A matter you do not take becomes a referral card with the reason attached.
  • →Opposing party — the field that runs the gate. Taken by name, with related parties, before a slot is offered. No other pack in the product has a check that blocks scheduling.
  • →Key dates, captured and never interpreted. Incident, service, hearing. The record holds them exactly as given; what they mean for a deadline is a solicitor's answer, not the AI's.
  • →Already represented? If they are, the conversation stops there and goes to a person — whether the firm may communicate with them at all is not a machine's call.
  • →Safe to leave a message? In family and domestic matters this is a safety field, not a convenience one. It is asked, recorded, and followed without exception.
  • →Matter-walled records. A record opened on one matter is not visible from another. Nothing here works like a clinic's single patient list, and that was the most expensive part to build.

A gate before a diary,
and nothing resembling advice.

Intake at a firm is a gate before it is a diary. The matter carries the opposing party so the conflict check can run, the jurisdiction that decides whether you may act at all, and a safe-to-contact flag that is a safety field rather than a preference. Fees come from your published rates. Merits, deadlines and valuations stay on a solicitor’s desk.

What the calendar enforces
  1. Nothing is confirmed until the conflict check clears. A time may be held; the gate is on the calendar, not on a checklist.
  2. A matter type that is not on the practice list never reaches the diary — it becomes a referral with the reason attached.
  3. Matters are walled: a record opened on one matter is not visible from another.
What a figure is quoted from
  • Consultation fee
  • Fixed-fee service from your list
  • Hourly rate, as published
  • Disbursements — named, never estimated
Who ends up with the work

A solicitor clears the conflict check and signs the engagement letter. Nothing binds the firm until they do.

Where it stops

Merits, deadlines, valuations, and anything that would amount to advice. Every one of those is a person.
