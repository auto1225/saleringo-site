The fields a venue actually needs

  • →Seasonal pricing rules. A peak Saturday in October and a Friday in February are not the same number — the quote applies the rule you wrote, not a guess.

The guest count decides the hall —
and a hold is never a booking.

A wedding record holds two names and one date, and then the number that rules everything else: the guest count. A hall below it is never offered however free the calendar looks — capacity is a fire limit, not a negotiation. One party per time slot per hall, enforced by the calendar rather than by whoever answered, and no outside supplier is logged without a current public-liability certificate on file. A quote is hall hire, the catering minimum for that hall, corkage and licence and production if taken, then tax, service charge and deposit. And a date can be held, never guaranteed — it says so in the same breath.

  2. One party per time slot per hall — the calendar enforces it, not the person.
  3. A date can be held, never confirmed, until someone signs it off.
  4. No outside supplier is logged without a current public-liability certificate on file.
What a quote is built from
  • Hall hire — $2,300 on a peak Saturday, $1,280 off-peak Friday
  • Catering minimum — the hall’s 150 covers at $50, payable whether 90 come or 150
  • Corkage, ceremony license fee and production, if taken
  • Tax, service charge, deposit and balance
