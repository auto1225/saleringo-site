and what it will not do.

Most AI receptionists hand you a transcript.
This one hands you an applicant file that is actually complete.

An applicant decides over a cycle, not an evening, and drops out at the document stage more often than at the offer stage. So the other half of Saleringo is a CRM shaped like an applicant file — programme, entry year, fee status as stated, documents outstanding, offer conditions — moving through the stages your office already works in.

The fields an admissions office actually needs

A generic CRM asks for a name.
This one asks which entry year.

  • →Every answer carries its source. The record stores which page a figure came from and when that page last changed. When an applicant says "your chatbot told me", you can open the line and read it.
  • →Documents, chased by name. Not "please complete your application" — the actual missing item, on the channel they read, timed against your deadline rather than a generic drip.
  • →Fee status as stated, never as assessed. The applicant tells you what they believe; the field records that it is unverified until your office rules on it. Two different things, two different colours.
  • →Refusals are records too. A refused question is written to the file with its wording intact and routed to a person, so nobody has to take the applicant's word for what was said.

Every deadline in two timezones —
and no word on the outcome.

An applicant record carries the programme and the entry year, because the rules change with the cycle, and it carries a fee status flagged as stated and unverified. A qualification is filed as a document, never a verdict. Every deadline is quoted in the applicant’s timezone as well as yours, a figure is only quotable when the page publishing it names this cycle’s entry year, and document reminders fire against your published closing dates rather than a generic timer. And no sentence here makes, implies or forecasts an admissions decision, or interprets an immigration rule: the officer rules on the qualification, your advice team takes the visa.

What the calendar enforces
  1. Every deadline is quoted in the applicant's timezone as well as yours — a deadline stated in one timezone is half an answer.
  2. A figure is only quotable if the page publishing it names the entry year on this page's cycle.
  3. Document reminders fire against your published closing dates, never on a generic timer.
What a fee answer is built from
  • Tuition for that year and that fee status
  • Deposit and when it is due
  • Published funding, with its closing date
  • What the fee does not include, stated plainly
Who ends up with the work

An admissions officer assesses the qualification and rules on fee status; your advice team takes anything touching immigration.

Where it stops

Any statement that makes, implies or forecasts an admissions decision — or that interprets an immigration rule.
