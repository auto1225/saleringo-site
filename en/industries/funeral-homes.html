Measured by a regulator, not by us

In the United States, telephone price disclosure
is not a courtesy. It is the law.

The FTC’s Funeral Rule requires a provider to give accurate price information from its General Price List to anyone who telephones and asks. In 2023 the Commission ran its first undercover phone sweep to find out whether that actually happens — and it called during business hours and outside them, which is the part that matters here.

Calling during business hours
7%

Staff could not obtain price information from 21 of the 278 providers called. Most homes, most of the time, answer the question properly when someone is at the desk.

Calling outside business hours
26%

The same request, after hours, failed at 73 of the 278 providers. Nothing about those homes changed between the two calls except who was there to pick up.

FTC staff called 278 randomly selected funeral providers between February and December 2023, in metropolitan and micropolitan areas, asking for prices for direct cremation, cremation with a memorial, and cremation with a viewing. After hours, staff had to call more than once or wait for a callback at nearly 70% of providers. 39 funeral homes received warning letters.

Eight fields, and one of them is a gate

A generic CRM asks who is calling.
This one asks whether they may instruct.

  • →Authority to instruct is a gate, not a field. Relationship to the deceased is established first, and nothing is arranged with someone who is not entitled to instruct. A grandson may be the one who calls; he is not automatically the one who decides.
  • →Place of death routes the transfer. Home, hospital, hospice or care home decides the procedure, which vehicle goes, and whether a release is needed before anyone can attend at all.

One product, several jurisdictions

The Funeral Rule is United States law.
This page is read everywhere.

So it is worth being exact about what applies where. What Saleringo does is the same everywhere: it reads the price list your firm published, as published. What that list must contain, and who may demand it over the telephone, is a matter for your jurisdiction and your own advisers.

In the United StatesThe FTC Funeral Rule makes telephone price disclosure from the General Price List a legal duty. Answering the phone at 4 AM and reading the line asked for is that duty being met, not a convenience.
Everywhere elseNo equivalent federal rule is assumed. The AI reads the price list you published under whatever local price-transparency rules govern it — and it will not read a figure that is not on that list.
In every caseThis is not legal advice and it is not a compliance certification. Your counsel signs off your price list and your disclosures, exactly as they do today. We are supplying the phone, not the opinion.

Does this help us comply with the FTC Funeral Rule?

In the United States the Funeral Rule requires a provider to give accurate price information from its General Price List to anyone who telephones and asks. Saleringo answers the phone at any hour and reads the GPL line the caller asked for, as written, then sends the full list. That is the mechanism the rule is about. It is not a compliance certification and it is not legal advice — your own counsel signs off your price list and your disclosures. Outside the United States the Funeral Rule does not apply and your local rules govern; the AI still reads only the list you published.

Will it try to sell a family a more expensive service?

No, and the restriction is built in rather than configured. It reads the figure the family asked for, in the order they asked, from your General Price List. It never suggests a costlier option, never says “most families choose”, and never volunteers an item nobody asked about. If a family asks which package they should have, it declines and says the director will go through the options with them.

What happens on a first call in the middle of the night?

It answers, establishes who is calling and whether they are entitled to instruct, records the place of death and whether the death has been certified, and pages your director on call with those details already written down. It gives the family your own out-of-hours number, unedited. It does not make a medical, legal or coroner determination, and it does not counsel.

How is family information handled?

Conversations capture contact and arrangement details — caller, relationship and authority to instruct, place of death, disposition, service type, pre-need status, preferred conference time. Records are kept separate from every other firm, encrypted in transit and at rest, with retention and deletion controls your firm operates. Full details, including data regions and subprocessors, are in the Security & data.

The arrangement configuration, written out

Authority first,
then a price list,
then a person.

A first call is not an inquiry. Nothing is arranged until authority to instruct is established; the place of death decides which transfer is even possible tonight; and a figure is only ever read off the General Price List you filed, line by line. That is the configuration set out below — together with the subjects, recommending and upgrading and counselling, that never leave a director’s desk.
