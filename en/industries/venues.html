Venue FAQ

Questions venues ask us.

Our inquiry volume triples in peak season. Can it keep up?

Yes. Plans are usage-based, so a busy month simply uses more of your conversation allowance. You get alerts at 80% and 100% of your limit, and you can raise it or set a hard cap at any time — we never silently stop answering your couples.

We already use a CRM. Do we have to switch?

No — keep it. Zapier and CSV export are live today, so every lead Saleringo captures can flow into the system you already run. Venue and hospitality tools are on the integration roadmap, and founding customers vote on what ships next. Saleringo also includes its own lead inbox if you would rather start light.

Can it handle international couples in their own language?

Yes. Answers come from one approved fact base and are delivered in the couple's language — a couple inquiring at 2 AM and another messaging from a different continent the same night both receive the same approved information, each in their own words.

What happens when a couple asks something we never wrote down?

It says it does not know and offers to bring in a person, then logs the gap. Your team sees the unanswered question in the inbox and can approve a new fact in a click — so the fact base grows from real inquiries instead of guesswork.
