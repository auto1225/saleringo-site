The course is not the problem

Your course sells itself.
The phone is what loses the day.

A visiting four-ball rings three clubs on a Friday night and plays at whichever one answers with a price and a time. Nobody rings back a club that let it go to an answerphone, and nobody drives twenty miles on a maybe.

See the 6:40 AM call  →

A pro shop counter staffed 07:30 to 18:00 is covered for 10½ hours a day — and that is the summer figure, with somebody standing there rather than out on the range or in the shop teaching. Across a full week the phone has a person beside it for 73 of 168 hours. The frost call in the headline above landed in the other 95.

This is not a statistic we collected. It is what your own opening hours divide into.

What it will not do

The course belongs to the greenkeeper.
The sheet belongs to the shop.

Some calls belong to the people who run the club and always will. These limits ship with the product; they are not a setting someone can switch off.

✕
Say the course is open

It reads the status your greens staff wrote, with the time on it. If the field is empty it says the field is empty. It never fills it in.

✕
Invent a tee time

It offers what the sheet has and nothing else. No slot squeezed between two groups, no promise your starter has to break at the first tee.

✕
Give a member rate to a non-member

Guest and member bands need a named member. Anyone else gets the visitor rate and the question goes to the shop.

✕
Rule on a handicap or a Rule of Golf

Cards, competitions, handicap queries and drops go to the committee or the professional, with the question intact.

Club FAQ

Questions clubs ask us.

Our green fee traffic triples between May and September. Can it keep up?

Yes. Plans are usage-based, so a busy month simply uses more of your conversation allowance. You get alerts at 80% and 100% of your limit, and you can raise it or set a hard cap at any time — we never silently stop answering your golfers.

We already run a tee-sheet system. Do we have to replace it?

No, and we would rather tell you plainly what that means today. Saleringo does not yet plug straight into golf tee-sheet software. Zapier and CSV export are live, so every enquiry it captures reaches the system you already run, and the AI works from a synced view of your sheet rather than writing into it.
