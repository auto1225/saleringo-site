Where the AI stops quoting

Some calls are not a price.
They are a survey you have not done yet.

A number given on a Tuesday evening and withdrawn on Thursday costs more than the job was worth. So the refusals below ship enabled. They are not a confidence threshold and they are not a setting anyone can switch off to hit a booking target.

Hard stop No firm price without the survey

Where your own rules require a pre-move survey, the AI will not produce a figure — not a ballpark, not a "from", not "roughly". It says which fact is missing, and offers the next two survey slots.

Hard stop No crew and no date that is not in the diary

Friday the 26th is either free in your diary or it is not. The AI reads what is there and offers that. It never holds a date on goodwill, and it never names a crew that is booked elsewhere.

Hard boundary No specialist item accepted, no cover level invented

Pianos, safes, aquariums, gym rigs, mowers with fuel in them: written down by name, put to a person, never agreed to on the phone. And it quotes no insurance limit you have not given it.

STEP 01DetectA price request that your rules say needs a survey, or an item on your specialist list, flips the call out of quoting mode.
STEP 02Say so plainlyOne sentence, in wording you approved: what is missing and why the number would be wrong. No stalling, no "our team will be in touch".
STEP 03Book the survey insteadReal slots from the surveyor's diary, video or in-person, so the caller leaves the call with a time rather than a promise.
STEP 04Leave a trailRecording, transcript and the reason it refused land on the survey card for the morning review.

Removals firms ask us

Will it give a caller a price over the phone?

Only where your own rules allow one. A single item or a small part-load can be priced from your list. A house move with a floor, a lift, a permit street and a piano in it does not get a number from the AI — it gets a pre-move survey in the diary, which is the thing the call was actually for.

Do you integrate with Moveware, MoveMan or Voxme?

Not directly, and we are not going to pretend otherwise. Today the survey card and the enquiry land in Saleringo's own inbox and CRM, and push out through Zapier or CSV export. Direct removals-software links are on the roadmap and founding-cohort members vote on the order.

The chain slips and the keys are late. What does it say?

It reads the waiting-time rate off your rate card and says the number out loud. It does not promise the crew can stay, because the crew has another job at four — that decision goes to your move manager, with the job and the diary attached.

Can it handle a piano, a safe or a full home gym?

It writes them down by name and puts them in front of a person. It never accepts a specialist or prohibited item on the phone, and it never tells a caller their gas bottles or paint tins will go on the van.

Can I start with evenings and weekends only?

That is how most firms begin. Forward the line after 5 PM and at weekends, read the transcripts for a week, then widen the window once the answers sound like your firm. Reversing it is one forwarding change on your side; there is no ticket to raise.
