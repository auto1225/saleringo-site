The fields a stay actually needs

A generic CRM asks for a name.
This one asks what time they land.

  • →Availability and rates in the guest's language. One rate table, one policy set, answered in whichever language they wrote in.
  • →Direct-booking capture. Every inquiry answered before an OTA gets there is a booking with no commission attached — and the report shows you how many.
  • →Pre-arrival messages that send themselves. Directions, check-in window, late-arrival instructions — queued from the booking, in their language.
  • →Held, never sold. The AI can quote and hold. Confirming a room is your manager's decision, every time.

Dates, party, pet —
and the answer it never gives.

A stay is a date range, a party and a pet, in that order, because each one narrows the units that qualify. The rules run at unit level, not property level: nothing under the party size is offered, a pet-friendly unit is quoted with its fee in the same sentence, and a late arrival gets the self-check-in instructions before they think to ask. Rates come from your channel manager. Whether a room is free is a sentence only a person says.

Who ends up with the work

Housekeeping is told about the late arrival and the pet before check-in day.

Where it stops

Confirming a rate the channel manager has not released, or taking payment.
