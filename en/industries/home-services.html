Where the AI stops talking

Some calls
are not a booking problem.

Gas, smoke, a sparking panel — these never get "handled" by software. Saleringo is built to recognize them, stop selling, and put a person on it. These limits ship enabled; they are not settings someone can switch off to hit a booking target.

Immediate handoff Gas smell or suspected leak

Booking stops mid-sentence. The caller hears your approved safety script — leave the area, call emergency services if the smell is strong — while the on-call tech is paged with the address already captured.

Immediate handoff Smoke, sparks, burning smell

Electrical fire risk routes to a human on the spot. The AI never decides that a described symptom is "probably fine" — it escalates and says so plainly to the caller.

Hard boundary No DIY, no fixed totals

It won't walk a caller through opening a breaker panel, and it won't quote a final repair total before your tech has seen the unit. Call-out fee and approved ranges only.

FAQ

Shops like yours ask us

A caller says they smell gas. What exactly happens?

The AI stops booking immediately. It follows your approved safety script — advising the caller to leave the area and call emergency services if the smell is strong — and pages your on-call tech at the same moment, with the address and details already captured. It never decides on its own that a situation is safe.

Do you integrate with ServiceTitan, Jobber, or Housecall Pro?

Not yet — and we won't pretend otherwise. Those integrations are on the roadmap, and founding-cohort members vote on the build order. Today, every call lands in Saleringo's built-in inbox and CRM, and jobs push out through Zapier or CSV export.

Can it quote repair prices over the phone?

It quotes exactly what you approve: call-out fee, diagnostic fee and price ranges. It never gives a fixed total before your tech has inspected the unit — the final number stays with the human holding the flashlight.

Does it handle Spanish-speaking callers?

Yes. The US founding cohort launches with English and Spanish — the AI detects the caller's language from the first sentence and answers in it. Job notes still reach your team in English, so dispatch never slows down.

Can I start with nights only?

That's how most shops begin. Forward your line after 6 PM and on weekends, listen to the recordings for a week, then widen the window when the answers sound like your business. Switching back is a forwarding change on your side, not a support ticket.
