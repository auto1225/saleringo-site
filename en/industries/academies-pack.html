The fields an academy actually needs

A generic CRM asks for a name.
This one asks what year they're in.

  • →Trial booking, on the spot. The parent is offered real openings from your timetable and leaves the conversation with a class and a reminder, not a callback promise.
  • →Level-test scheduling. Only the slots and capacities you opened. Assigning the level afterwards stays with your director — the AI never places a student.
  • →Term-enrolment reminders. Re-enrolment, unpaid tuition and the end-of-term nudge fire on schedule, on the channel the parent actually uses.
  • →Schedule fit, written down. "Weekday evenings only, not Wednesdays" is a field, not a note somebody loses. Siblings sit on the same parent record.

Age, level, and the one promise
it will never make.

A parent ringing a tutoring centre is buying a placement, not an appointment. So the record carries age and last grade before it carries a time. The real roster cap decides whether a trial seat exists. A quote is the term fee plus registration plus your sibling rule. The one sentence it will never say is that a child will get a grade.

What the record carries
Parent name and numberthe payer is the contact
Student name and agedecides which class
Level or last gradeplacement, not a guess
Subjectthe actual course
Days and times they can attendthe real constraint
Trial or enrolmenta different next step
What the calendar enforces
  1. Age and level together decide the class — neither alone is enough to book.
  2. A trial seat is held against the real roster cap, not an approximate one.
  3. Term dates and fees come from your calendar, never from the model.
What a quote is built from
  • Term fee for the placed class
  • Registration or materials
  • Sibling or multi-term rule from your list
  • What is not included, stated plainly
Who ends up with the work

Your director confirms the placement level before the trial is fixed.

Where it stops

Promising a result, a grade, or a place before a person checks the roster.

Every class, roster cap and term fee shown here is read from the schedule you upload. Those four are the ninety-day test — the same four we would watch if we were running the academy.
