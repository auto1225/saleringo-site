FAQ

Hoteliers ask us

Our guests write in five different languages. Can it keep up?

Yes — Saleringo detects the guest's language and answers in it, across 30+ languages. Your facts live in one place in your language; the AI handles translation both ways, and your inbox shows the conversation with an English summary alongside.

Does it connect to our PMS?

Not yet, and we will not pretend otherwise. Today Saleringo syncs holds to Google or Outlook Calendar, exports guest records as CSV, and pushes events through Zapier. Mews and Cloudbeds are on the roadmap and founding customers vote on the build order. Until then every booking request is held for your manager's confirmation — nothing is auto-confirmed.

Do you take a commission on bookings?

No. Saleringo is a flat monthly subscription with no commission. See the pricing page for plans. Every direct booking it wins is entirely yours — that is the whole point of answering guests before an OTA does.

Can it take deposits or card payments?

It never touches card details. Once your manager confirms a booking, the AI can send your existing payment or deposit link and follow up if it goes unpaid — collecting the money stays with the tools you already trust.

What stops it from overbooking a room?

It has no authority to confirm. The AI can quote a rate, describe a policy and hold a request, but availability is treated as unknown until a person on your team says otherwise. Read the Trust Center for the full rule set.
