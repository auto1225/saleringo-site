The fields a studio actually needs

A generic CRM asks for a name.
This one asks whether the form came back.

  • →Capacity is a number, not a mood. Ten beds means ten. A full class gets a numbered waitlist place and the caller is told the number — because an eleventh booking is a person standing in the doorway at 6:14.
  • →The trial as a booking, not a lead. A trial enquiry that ends in "someone will call you back" is a trial at another studio. It ends in a named class, a held place and a form on its way.
  • →The health answer as a stop. Not a note for somebody to read later. The words go down verbatim, an instructor is attached, and nothing resting on that question is confirmed.
  • →Membership admin off the desk's day. Freezes, upgrades, notice periods, the debit date: the rule is read as written and the request is logged with a timestamp. The deciding stays with you.
See the pipeline it fills

A waitlist place, not an eleventh bed —
and one subject it stops dead.

A studio record turns on capacity and a form. The class sets the length and the number of beds; whether the caller is a trial, a drop-in or a member decides which price is even quoted; the instructor asked for by name decides which timetable is read. A full class offers a numbered waitlist place, never a bed that does not exist, and a first session stays held — said out loud as held — while the screening form is outstanding. The late-cancel fee is named before the booking, not after it is missed. And injuries, conditions, pregnancy, medication, nutrition and how to train are subjects the AI says out loud it cannot answer, every time — as are cancelling a membership and waiving a fee.

What the timetable enforces
  1. A class at capacity offers a numbered waitlist place, never a bed. There is no eleventh bed to give away.
  2. A first session stays held while the screening form is outstanding — and the caller is told which it is.
  3. The cancellation window and its fee are said before the booking is taken, not after it is missed.
What a quote is built from
  • The class or session price on your own list
  • The trial offer, stated exactly as written
  • The membership rate and the direct debit date
  • The late-cancel and no-show fee, named before the booking
Who ends up with the work

The instructor opens the class list with the trial's name, their experience, and anything they said about a body already written down.

Where it stops

Injuries, conditions, pregnancy, medication, nutrition and how to train — and cancelling a membership or waiving a fee.
