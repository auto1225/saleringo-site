FAQ

Dental clinics ask us

Does Saleringo integrate with our practice management system?

Keep your CRM and PMS — Zapier and CSV export are live today, so records flow into the systems you already run. Direct PMS integrations (Dentrix, Open Dental) are on the roadmap, and founding customers vote on the build order. Every conversation also lands in Saleringo’s built-in inbox with bookings on its own scheduler, so your front desk can reconcile in minutes, not hours.

What can it say about insurance?

Only what you approve — for example, which plans you accept and what to bring to a first visit. It captures the patient’s insurance details during booking, but it never estimates coverage or benefits; those questions route to your front desk with the details already collected.

How do recall campaigns work?

Recall and no-show reminders queue automatically from your CRM records — overdue hygiene visits, unconfirmed appointments, post-treatment check-ins. Patients confirm or rebook right in the chat, and every response updates the record.

What happens with a night-time emergency?

Urgent-pain messages skip the queue. The AI stops answering questions, shares your approved emergency instructions, and routes the patient to your on-call line immediately — it never handles an emergency alone.
