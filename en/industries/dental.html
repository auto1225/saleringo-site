The work it readies

  • Reconcile the morning’s bookings → your front desk → from Saleringo’s inbox and CSV export — minutes, not hours.
  • Run the coverage check → your front desk → queued with the plan and member ID already collected, flagged for first thing Monday.
  • Call back on the treatment plan → the treatment coordinator → the day-10 follow-up fires automatically; the callback is booked with the reason attached.
  • Take the urgent call → the on-call dentist → routed inside the same call, the record flagged urgent, first on the morning list.

What stays a human decision

  • Anything clinical. Diagnosis, “is this normal”, pain scores, medication — refused in any wording; symptoms go to a person.
  • What a plan will pay. It captures the plan and member ID, but a benefits estimate is never given — your desk runs the check.
  • Prices and payment terms. No discounts, no quote-matching, no improvised installments — the published range is stated and the decision goes to a person.

FAQ

Dental clinics ask us

Does Saleringo integrate with our practice management system?

Keep your CRM and PMS — Zapier and CSV export are live today, so records flow into the systems you already run. Direct PMS integrations (Dentrix, Open Dental) are on the roadmap; founding customers vote on the build order. Every conversation also lands in Saleringo’s built-in inbox, with bookings on its own scheduler — your front desk reconciles in minutes, not hours.

What can it say about insurance?

Only what you approve — for example, which plans you accept and what to bring to a first visit. It captures the patient’s insurance details during booking, but it never estimates coverage or benefits; those questions route to your front desk with the details already collected.

How do recall campaigns work?

Recall and no-show reminders queue automatically from your CRM records — overdue hygiene visits, unconfirmed appointments, post-treatment check-ins. Patients confirm or rebook right in the chat, and every response updates the record.

What happens with a night-time emergency?

Urgent-pain messages skip the queue. The AI stops answering questions, shares your approved emergency instructions, and routes the patient to your on-call line immediately — it never handles an emergency alone.
