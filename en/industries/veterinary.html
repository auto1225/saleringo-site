What actually arrives, and when

Half of it is a booster.
The other half is a person
who should not be talking to software.

No other trade on this site has a sharper line running through its inbox. On one side sit the questions a practice answers forty times a week — hours, boosters, nail clips, what a dental costs, whether you see rabbits. On the other sit toxicity, dosage, symptoms and end-of-life, where a confident answer from a machine is worse than no answer at all. A veterinary AI is only worth putting on your main line if it can tell the two apart every single time, out loud, in the first sentence.

The fields a practice actually needs

Species and weight are not
optional metadata here.

  • →The species decides who can see it. A rabbit, a snake and a horse do not go on the same list. The AI only offers clinicians and clinics you have marked as seeing that species — and says so plainly if none is free.
  • →The weight band decides the price. Anaesthetic work is priced by weight in almost every practice, so it is priced by weight here too: your bands, your figures, always a range until the vet has examined the animal.
  • →Recalls fire off the animal's own record. Boosters, post-op checks, parasite treatment. The reminder goes out on the channel the owner last used, and the reply lands back on the same record.
  • →A Monday number, not a feeling. Inquiries, bookings taken out of hours, recalls answered, and how many conversations left the AI for a person — per branch and per clinician, exportable to PDF or Excel.

FAQ

Veterinary practices ask us

Will it ever give clinical advice?

No, and not as a setting you could switch on. Toxicity, dosage, symptom assessment, "should I be worried", medication and end-of-life questions are refused categorically. The AI says so in the first sentence, routes the caller to the number you nominated, and writes the record so your team is not starting cold. It books, quotes and reminds. It does not assess.

Does it connect to our practice-management system?

Not directly — whichever one you run, treat it as a bridge. Records reach it through Zapier or a CSV export your desk imports, and every conversation also lands in Saleringo's own inbox with bookings on its own scheduler. A bridge means somebody does a step. Be honest with yourself about whether your desk will do that daily, because if the answer is no, this is not ready for you yet.

What happens with an out-of-hours emergency?

It stops answering questions. The caller gets the out-of-hours instructions and the number you nominated — your own on-call line, or the partner hospital you entered — in the first sentence, with your staff alerted and a record created so the follow-up actually happens. It never handles an emergency alone and it never tries to work out how urgent something is.

Can it quote prices?

Only figures your vets have approved, and for anaesthetic work only as a weight-banded range, confirmed after the animal has been examined. If a price is not on the approved list, the AI says it cannot quote it and books the appointment that produces the real figure. Nothing is ever improvised, rounded or averaged.

Can it see the animal's record — boosters, weight, history?

It works from the record held in Saleringo, which is built up from the conversations themselves and from anything you import at onboarding. That is what lets it read a booster due date instead of asking for it. Where your patient software is the source of truth, the bridge above is how the two stay aligned, and we would rather you knew that up front.

Can it handle repeat prescriptions?

It can take the request, attach the record and put it in front of a vet immediately, with the detail that matters — how much medication is left — captured. It cannot name a drug, confirm a quantity, suggest timing or authorise anything. Repeats are a veterinary decision and they stay one.

We run two branches and refer to a hospital. How does routing work?

Each branch is its own site inside one account, with its own number, hours, clinicians, species list and price sheet. Referral and out-of-hours partners are entered by you as named destinations with their own instructions. The AI cannot invent a partner and will say it does not know rather than guess — see branches and referral partners above.
