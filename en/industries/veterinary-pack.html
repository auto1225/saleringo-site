The fields a practice actually needs

Species and weight
are not optional metadata here.

  • →The species decides who can see it. A rabbit, a snake and a horse do not go on the same list. The AI only offers clinicians and clinics you have marked as seeing that species — and says so plainly if none is free.
  • →The weight band decides the price. Anaesthetic work is priced by weight in almost every practice, so it is priced by weight here too: your bands, your figures, always a range until the vet has examined the animal.
  • →Recalls fire off the animal's own record. Boosters, post-op checks, parasite treatment. The reminder goes out on the channel the owner last used, and the reply lands back on the same record.
  • →A Monday number, not a feeling. Inquiries, bookings taken out of hours, recalls answered, and how many conversations left the AI for a person — per branch and per clinician, exportable to PDF or Excel.
See the pipeline it fills

The animal first, the diary second,
and the clinic left alone.

A practice record starts with the animal, not the appointment: species and breed, the owner’s own words for the symptom kept verbatim, the vaccination status that changes what may be booked at all. Appointment type sets the slot length, exactly as your practice has it configured. A consult fee is read off your species list with the out-of-hours surcharge said out loud. Everything clinical stops here and goes to a vet.

What the calendar enforces
  1. Appointment type decides the slot length — a nail clip and a lameness work-up are not the same booking, whatever the animal weighs.
  2. Subjects on your refusal list — poison, dosage, symptoms, end-of-life — leave the booking flow at once and go to the out-of-hours instructions you wrote. It is matching the subject, not judging the urgency.
  3. A first visit for an unvaccinated animal is flagged before it is confirmed.
What a quote is built from
  • Consultation fee by species
  • Vaccination or procedure from your list
  • Medication — only if already priced
  • Out-of-hours surcharge, stated up front
