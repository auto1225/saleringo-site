The Dry cleaning & laundry pack

Everything in the pack,
and everything it will not do.

The CRM fields and stages that follow every call, what it connects to, and what it never says. Why any of it matters is on the Dry cleaning & laundry page.

What it connects to

These connect the same way in every trade. Whether your booking system or practice software connects depends on the product, so we check and tell you — we do not write “yes” first.

  • Phone lineKeep the main number you already publish; forward only the calls you cannot take, or let the AI answer from the first ring. When it needs to, it hands the call to a person.
  • WhatsApp & messengersMessages to your business number are answered from the same facts. Templates and follow-ups included.
  • Website chatOne line of script on the site you already have. No rebuild.
  • CalendarBookings are written into Google Calendar or Outlook as real slots, not notes.
  • Text messagesConfirmations and day-before reminders, with a delivery record.
  • ExportCustomers, conversations and recordings can be downloaded in full at any time — CSV and webhooks.

What it will not do

Not a setting you switch on and off. These are blocked when the Dry cleaning & laundry answering is built; anything that touches them goes to a person instead of being answered.

  • It never guarantees a stain will come out.Wine, oil and ink depend on fabric and time. It stops at “the owner will look and tell you upfront.”
  • It never promises a rush without seeing the day’s load.Only the standard turnaround is quoted. Whether a rush is possible is decided by the owner who knows what is on the rail.
  • It never rules on a damage claim.A shrunk sweater or faded shirt is a matter for the owner and the shop’s policy. The facts are written down and passed on.
  • It never accepts designer or leather pieces by phone.The owner reads the care label and decides whether it can be cleaned. It is logged as an item to check at intake.

This page is the list; the argument is on the other one. What kind of call comes in at night, what a missed one costs, and why a machine must not say certain things are written on the page this pack came from.
