What it will not do.

A person is always behind it

Every sentence traces back
to a page you published.

Fees, deadlines, entry criteria, accepted tests, accommodation, what to send and where — each loaded from a named prospectus or policy page, approved before go-live, and re-checked when that page changes. If a page has not been updated for the new cycle, the AI says so rather than reading last year's number in this year's voice.

Decisions, eligibility, visas and anything about a named applicant's file never reach it at all. Those land with your officers, with the full conversation and the documents attached. That is the whole trade: the 02:40 questions come off the desk, and the judgement stays where it belongs.

Admissions FAQ

Asked by registrars.

How do we know an answer came from our own pages?

Because every answer stores its source and the date that source last changed, and the applicant is sent the link. When a dispute lands — "your website said the deadline was the fifteenth" — you open the conversation and see which page was quoted and what it said at that moment. You are arguing about a record, not a memory. A question with no approved source is a handoff, not a paraphrase.

What happens on results day and during Clearing?

Volume spikes are the case for this, not the exception. It answers every caller at once and takes grades, UCAS number and subject before a tutor picks up — the human conversation starts at minute two. It makes no offers and tells no one their chances.

Plans include monthly conversation allowances with alerts at 80% and 100%, so you add capacity before August, not during it. We never silently stop answering your applicants.
