The Public sector pack

Everything in the pack,
and what it will not do.

The CRM behind the call, the fields and stages this trade actually works in, and the systems it hands to. The argument for any of it is on the Public sector page.

Most AI receptionists hand you a transcript.
This one hands you a contact record with a source.

A message taken is not a record. The other half of Saleringo is a CRM built around one contact — service area, location, category, reference number, the language it was held in, and the page the answer came from. All of it moves through the stages your authority already works in, with every step timestamped.

Nine fields, and one of them is the source

A generic CRM stores what was said.
This one stores where it came from.

  • →Service area, from your own list. Waste, council tax, housing, highways, planning, environmental health, licensing. The routing is the record — a contact with no service area attached is an unrouted contact, and those are the ones that come back.
  • →Out-of-hours category — the field that pages a person. Matched against the list your authority wrote. An unmatched night call is escalated rather than parked, and the record says which of the two happened.
  • →Location, captured and never tidied. The street, the side, the house number, the landmark the caller used. A crew sent to the wrong end of a long road is a paraphrase problem.
  • →Reference number, issued and read out. Before the call ends, in your own format, and repeated in writing on the channel they used. It is what stops the third call about the same thing.
  • →Language of the contact. Recorded, so the callback is not made in the wrong one and so you can see, at the end of a quarter, which languages your main line actually took.
  • →Source of the answer, on every answer. The page or document it came from and the date your authority last approved it. Refusals are stored the same way, because a refusal is the line most likely to be asked about later.

Every answer traceable to a published page —
and no decision, ever.

A contact record here carries a service area from your own list, the out-of-hours category that decides whether a person is woken, and the source of every answer given: page, version and approval date. Only the categories your authority named page a duty officer; an unmatched night call goes to your on-call contact and is flagged, never quietly filed. Every logged contact leaves with a reference number read out loud and the sentence “no decision has been made”. Figures come from published fees, the published collection calendar, published hours and published service standards — nowhere else. Eligibility, entitlements, rulings and interpretations are a person, every time.

What the triage enforces
  1. Only categories your authority named page a duty officer. An unmatched night call is escalated to your on-call contact and flagged, never quietly filed.
  2. Every logged contact leaves with a reference number and the sentence "no decision has been made".
  3. Nothing may be said that does not trace to a published page with an approval date. Everything else is a handoff.
What a figure is quoted from
  • Published fees & charges
  • Published collection calendar
  • Published opening hours
  • Published service standards — where they exist, never invented
Who ends up with the work

A duty officer for the matched emergencies; a named service and a named officer for everything else, next working day.

Where it stops

Eligibility, entitlements, rulings, interpretations, and anything that would amount to a decision. Every one of those is a person.

Those are numbers a service manager already reports; the only thing that changes is who was on the line when the phone rang at 1:40 AM.

This is the manifest, not the argument. Why any of it matters — the call that arrives at night, what it costs to miss, what the AI refuses to say — is on the page this came from.
