The line the whole page rests on

It will not promise a date
the carrier has not given.

Not as "should be fine". Not as "usually two days". Not if the buyer says it is for a birthday, and not if they ask a third time. It reads the last scan and the carrier's own estimate, leaves the word estimate in, and stops. Everything after that is the carrier's sentence to write, not ours and not yours.

If that sounds like it will cost you a calm conversation tonight: it is the same sentence that stops one reassurance at 11:40 PM becoming a chargeback on Monday, and it is the reason this can sit on your front line at all. It ships enabled. It is not a setting anybody can switch off to close a conversation faster.

Refused outright "Will it definitely be here by Saturday?"

No delivery date is invented, in any wording. The last scan, the place and the time are read back; the carrier's estimate is quoted as an estimate; the carrier's own tracking page is offered.

Refused outright "It's day 34 — refund me anyway?"

The published window is the window, and the AI neither stretches it nor tightens it. Outside it, nothing is decided: the order, the dates, the reason, the photos and what the buyer is actually asking for are gathered and handed over. A goodwill exception is a commercial decision, and a person makes it.

Hard boundary No duty figure, no invented stock, no card numbers

Three things it will not produce out of thin air: a customs or tax figure, a stock level it cannot read from your record, and a place for anyone to type a card number. It states the terms you sell on, says plainly when it cannot see stock, and sends people to your own checkout.

STEP 01Find the order firstOrder number, or the email it was placed with. No order, no status — and no guessing from a first name.
STEP 02Read, do not inferThe last carrier scan and the carrier's own estimate, quoted with the word estimate still in the sentence.
STEP 03Apply the published policyWindow, condition, who pays the postage. Word for word from what you published — never extended, never quietly tightened.
STEP 04Hand the exception overOutside policy, damaged, missing, or anything about money moving: gathered in full, then passed to a person with the work already done.

Why the refusals are drawn where they are. Selling at a distance is heavily regulated; the rules belong to regulators, not to us. In the US, the FTC's Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule (16 CFR Part 435) requires shipping within the advertised time — 30 days if none advertised — or the buyer's consent, or a refund.

In the EU, the Consumer Rights Directive (2011/83/EU) gives a consumer 14 days to withdraw from a distance contract. The UK's Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 say the same. Since 1 July 2021 the EU charges import VAT on goods of any value — the old €22 relief is gone. How answers get approved →

The other side of the message

An order number is not admin.
It is the difference between

With it, the whole thing is a lookup that ends in one sentence. Without it, it is four exchanges, a half-remembered surname and somebody guessing which of two Sarahs ordered the green one. That is why it is asked for in the first line, not the fifth.

Step 03The carrier's last scan Where it was, when, and what the carrier itself estimates. Read back, never rounded up into a promise.

The marketplace listing does not quote

A marketplace or shopping-mall storefront runs on the platform's returns rules, not yours, and a second-country domain runs on its own currency and its own delivery services. Each storefront is set up as its own store inside one account, and the AI never crosses between them.

01Its own policy, in its own words

A buyer who bought on the marketplace hears the marketplace's window and the marketplace's postage rule. Never your site's version, in either direction — and that is the single most common way a support answer becomes a platform case.

02Its own currency, delivery table and cut-off

The delivery price and the cut-off time read out on the German store are the German store's, in euros, from the warehouse that actually ships it — not a converted version of the home one.

03Its own duty terms

Duties paid at checkout on one storefront and collected by the carrier on another is normal. The terms stated on the chat are always the ones belonging to the storefront the order was placed on. No figure is ever calculated.

FAQ

Stores like yours ask us

Will it tell a customer when the parcel will arrive?

It reads back what your record and the carrier already say: the last scan, where it happened, and the carrier's own estimate with the word estimate left in. It does not turn an estimate into a promise or produce a date of its own. That refusal ships enabled — not a setting anyone can switch off to close a conversation faster.

Can it approve a refund or a return outside our published policy?

No. It applies the policy you published — the window, the condition, who pays return postage — and neither extends nor tightens it. A request outside the policy is collected in full: order number, dates, reason, photos, and what the buyer is asking for. Then it goes to a person — a goodwill exception is a commercial decision, not one an AI should make on your behalf at midnight.

Does it know what is in stock?

Only if your stock is connected to it. If it can see the stock record it reads the record; if it cannot, it says it cannot and hands the question over. It never produces a stock figure it did not read from somewhere — because an invented yes becomes an oversell, a cancellation and a refund, in that order.

What does it do about customs, duties and import VAT?

It states which terms you sell on — duties paid at checkout, or collected by the carrier on delivery — and points at the page where the rest is published. It does not calculate a figure. Since 1 July 2021 the EU charges import VAT on commercial goods of any value, and the rules keep moving — exactly why nobody should improvise numbers on your behalf at 11 PM.

We sell in six countries. Does it answer in six languages?

It answers in the language the buyer wrote in; the same order record sits behind every language, in yours. What changes per storefront is the policy it reads: return window, who pays return postage, delivery services, currency, duty terms. Each storefront is configured separately, and the AI does not cross between them.
