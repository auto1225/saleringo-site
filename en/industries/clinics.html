Clinic questions

Asked by clinics like yours.

How does this fit medical advertising regulations?

Saleringo only says what your clinic approved — no outcome claims, no comparative claims, no improvised prices. Every answer traces to a reviewed fact with a source and expiry date, so your compliance review covers the answer set itself, not an unpredictable model. Anything outside the approved set is handed to your staff.

How is patient information handled?

Conversations capture contact and booking details — name, language, requested treatment, preferred dates — not clinical records. Data is kept separate from every other clinic, encrypted in transit and at rest, with retention and deletion controls your clinic operates. Full details, including data regions and subprocessors, are in the Security & data.

Which languages are supported, and how far does support go?

Website chat and WhatsApp work in the patient’s language, automatically detected. Support goes exactly as far as your approved answer sets: each language carries its own reviewed wording, and a language you haven’t approved gets a polite handoff to your team rather than a machine translation of a medical claim.

We see international patients who travel for care. Is that supported?

Yes — clinics serving patients across borders get the full cross-border toolkit: time-zone-aware answering, per-language approved answer sets, and WhatsApp threads that follow the patient home after the visit.
